\# The Brian Short Apps Platform Constitution



\*\*Artifact:\*\* CONST-001  

\*\*Version:\*\* 1.0  

\*\*Status:\*\* Stable  

\*\*Owner:\*\* Platform  



\## Preamble



The Brian Short Apps Platform exists to enable the creation of exceptional software through disciplined engineering, thoughtful design, and intentional systems.



> We are not building applications.  

> We are building the capability to build exceptional applications.



\## Core Articles



\### Article I — Purpose



The BSA Platform provides a shared engineering foundation that enables products to be built consistently, efficiently, and sustainably.



\### Article II — Systems Over Heroics



Exceptional software is the result of exceptional systems, not exceptional effort.



\### Article III — Platform Before Product



Applications solve domain-specific problems. The platform provides reusable capabilities.



\### Article IV — Simplicity



Simplicity is preferred over cleverness. Every abstraction must justify its existence.



\### Article V — Consistency



Consistency creates trust for users and reduces cognitive load for engineers.



\### Article VI — Documentation



Documentation is part of the product.



\### Article VII — Accessibility



Accessibility is a fundamental quality attribute.



\### Article VIII — Quality



Quality is designed into the platform through testing, automation, documentation, and review.



\### Article IX — Continuous Improvement



The platform evolves intentionally, with documented and versioned change.



\### Article X — Stewardship



Every engineer is a steward of the platform.
